How to Run the Flash and Torch Test and Confirm the Flash Fires

The torch test needs no app: Nothing you do in the check your phone's actual camera resolution ever leaves your device — the whole test runs locally in your browser tab.

  • Press the power on/off button with the lens pointed away from your eyes.
  • Watch the back of the phone, not the live preview. A flashlight test only passes when the LED itself lights.
  • Toggle it three times; a light that works only once is faulty too.

Camera Access, Permissions and Site Settings

Browsers control the flash through the camera, so the page asks for camera access first. Accept the permission prompt, or tap the camera icon in the address bar if you dismissed it. If you blocked it earlier, reset the camera permission:

  • Chrome desktop on Windows: Privacy & Security, then Site settings, then set Camera to Allow.
  • Android: Settings, Apps, your browser, Permissions, then Allow while using app.

Phone Flashlight Torch Control on Android Chrome and iOS Safari

No web page can flip the LED directly. The test opens the camera with getUserMedia, reads its capabilities with getCapabilities and looks for a torch property. If that capability exists, it calls applyConstraints with torch set to true. These standard web APIs come from the MediaStreamTrack spec, so support depends on the browser, not on how healthy your phone flashlight is: Nothing you do in the main camera test online ever leaves your device — the whole test runs locally in your browser tab.

Rear Camera vs Front Camera for the Torch

The flash LED sits beside the main lens, so test with the back camera. Front cameras almost never report a torch; a selfie "flash" is just display backlighting. On phones with several rear cameras, the browser may open a wide or zoom lens with no torch attached, so switch lenses first.

Reading Your Flashlight Test Result

If the LED stays dark, the error usually names the cause.

Torch Button Grayed Out or Disabled

A grayed out button means the browser never reported a torch, which is expected on iPhone, on the selfie lens and on laptops. The browser disabled it, not a broken LED, so retry in Chrome on Android with the rear lens.

Camera in Use or Not Readable Errors

This error means another app holds exclusive access to the camera. Close video calls, camera apps and any other browser tab using the lens, then reload.

Checking a Used Phone's Flash LED Before Paying

I'm standing in a café with a second-hand Android phone listed at $186, and the only question is whether to hand over the cash. The seller says the flash "works fine." The flashlight test on this page lets me check that in two minutes, without installing anything on someone else's phone.

I open the page in Chrome, accept the camera prompt and pick the rear lens. The torch button is active, so the browser can control the LED. Three manual toggles: on, on, then dark on the third press. One miss in three is not enough to walk away on, so I switch to SOS mode, because it toggles the LED far faster than my thumb can.

That turns a three-press check into a stress test of the torch: at a 0.2-second dot, SOS mode switches the LED on 153 times in two minutes, against my 3 manual presses. A slower dot gives fewer chances to catch a fault:

The LED drops 4 flashes, all in the second minute, while the phone is in my hand and the cable end of the case flexes slightly. That pattern reads like a loose connection rather than a burnt-out LED, and it is exactly the camera-module fault a bench test would confirm. I budget $64.90 for a replacement rear camera module, so I offer $121.10 and we settle at $122.

One caveat before trusting a dark LED at all: many phones block the flash when the battery is low or the body is hot. If the battery reads under 15%, a failed run means nothing. Charge to at least 20% and rerun the same LED check before you count any miss against the phone; here the battery showed 68%, so the 4 misses stand.

What a Dead LED Torch Means for Repair

If the button is active, permission is granted and the LED stays dark, software is ruled out. A dead flash LED usually points to a camera-module fault or a damaged connector, since on many phones the LED shares a board or flex cable with the camera. This is a self-check, not a full diagnosis: board-level faults need a bench test with proper diagnostics before any repair. Run two flashlight tests a few minutes apart; an LED flash light that works once and fails the next time is an intermittent fault worth reporting. A faint glow is a fail too; this flashlight / torch test covers hardware only.

Camera Flash Light Modes: Steady, Strobe and SOS

Once the LED passes, steady mode holds full brightness, strobe pulses it at a set strobe frequency, and the emergency SOS or beacon mode flashes three short, three long, three short. With a 0.2-second dot, a dash lasts 0.6 seconds and one SOS takes 5.4 seconds before a 1.4-second pause. Keep strobe slow around people with photosensitive epilepsy and use the auto-off timer.

White Screen Light When There Is No LED

With no usable torch, switch the light source to a white screen light: one bright screen color at full brightness. Far dimmer than a flash light, it still works on laptops and iPhones where torch control is blocked.

Privacy When You Test the Flashlight

This camera and flashlight test runs locally in your browser with no uploads. The video stays on your device, nothing is recorded, and the camera closes when you leave, so you can test the flashlight as often as you like without a privacy trade-off.

Resolution and Frame Rate in a Camera Test

The page also reads the stream with getSettings, which confirms the browser opened the rear-facing lens. The resolution and frame rate shown are what the camera driver negotiated with the browser, often below the sensor's physical maximum, so a lower number isn't a fault.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I test my phone's flashlight from a browser?
Yes, on Android. Chrome exposes torch control through the camera's MediaStreamTrack capabilities, so the page can switch the rear LED on and off once you allow camera access. iPhone browsers generally don't expose the torch.
Why is the torch button grayed out?
Your browser or the selected camera didn't report a torch. That's expected on iPhone, on front cameras and on most desktop browsers. Switch to the rear camera in Chrome on Android and try again.
Does the flash and torch test record anything?
No. The camera stream is used only to control the LED and stays on your device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
What does it mean if the LED never lights?
If the torch button is active and camera access is granted but the LED stays dark, the flash LED or camera module is likely faulty. A browser check is a quick self-check, so have a repair shop bench-test it.
Why does it say the camera is in use?
Another app or browser tab already has the camera. Close video calls, camera apps and other tabs using the camera, then reload the page.
How long is one SOS flash cycle?
In SOS mode each dot lasts 333 ms and each dash three times that. One S-O-S takes 27 units plus a 7-unit pause, about 11.3 seconds before it repeats.
What can I use if my phone has no flash?
Switch the light source to Screen Light. It fills the display with a bright colour you choose, which works on laptops and iPhones where the LED torch can't be controlled.
